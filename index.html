Frequently Asked Questions

1. How is the Suggestion Score calculated?

It penalizes heavy commute times (1.5x) and expensive fares (4.5x). Faster, cheaper routes score highest.

2. What does "Door-to-Door" mean?

We calculate full exact physical postcode distances and add a 12-15 minute flat buffer for walking to the specific platform.

3. Are these rent prices accurate?

Yes. Rent uses a geographical decay formula anchored to regional real estate averages, updated dynamically.

4. Why do you need my Gross Salary?

To calculate your true UK Take-Home Pay (after tax) to ensure affordability.

5. What is a good budget split?

Experts recommend spending max 40% of net income on living (rent + commute).

6. Does Transit Cost include weekends?

No. It multiplies peak single-fare by 2 (return), multiplied by selected office days.

7. How do Council Tax bands work?

The tooltip mathematically scales the base Borough 'Band D' rate so you know what you owe.

8. What if my office is outside London?

The engine automatically detects non-London postcodes and alerts you to explore local markets.
