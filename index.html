Frequently Asked Questions

1. How is the Suggestion Score calculated?

It penalizes heavy commute times (1.5x) and expensive fares (4.5x). Faster, cheaper routes score highest.

2. What does "Door-to-Door" mean?

We calculate full exact physical postcode distances and add a 12-15 minute flat buffer to account for walking to the specific transit line platform.

3. Are these rent prices accurate?

Yes. Rent uses a geographical decay formula anchored to regional real estate averages, updated dynamically for the current month and year.

4. Why do you need my Gross Salary?

To calculate your true UK Take-Home Pay (after tax). This ensures your recommendations are affordable.

5. What is a good budget split?

Experts recommend spending max 40% of net income on living (rent + utilities + commute).

6. Does Transit Cost include weekends?

No. It multiplies peak single-fare by 2 (return), multiplied by selected office days, across 4.33 weeks/month.

7. How do Council Tax bands work?

The tooltip mathematically scales the base Borough 'Band D' rate so you know exactly what you owe.

8. What if my office is outside London?

The engine automatically detects non-London postcodes and alerts you to explore local cost-effective markets instead of computing London routes.

9. Are utilities included in rent?

No. Budget an additional £150-£250 per month for council tax, energy, and water.

10. Can I save my favorite routes?

Yes! If you create an account and Sign In, you can securely save and pin specific neighborhoods.
